Our Story

Family recipes, comal-fired pupusas, and flavors from Cuscatlán.

We are here to share the flavors of El Salvador with the world. Our food is more than a meal — it is a connection to our roots, our traditions, and our culture. Every pupusa, every horchata, and every nuegado carries the warmth of family kitchens and the pride of a heritage worth preserving. We invite you to experience El Salvador through every bite.

We believe food is the best way to tell a story. Our dishes bring together bold flavors, fresh ingredients, and the spirit of El Salvador in every bite. Think of us as your passport to Salvadoran culture — vibrant, authentic, and unforgettable. Come hungry, leave inspired.

Pupusas

A handmade corn tortilla filled with savory goodness. Pupusas are El Salvador’s most beloved dish, stuffed with cheese, beans, pork, or a mix of flavors, then cooked to golden perfection on a griddle. Served with curtido (a tangy cabbage slaw) and tomato sauce, they are the perfect comfort food and the heart of Salvadoran cuisine.
