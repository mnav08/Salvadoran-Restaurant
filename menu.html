Pupusas

A handmade corn tortilla filled with savory goodness. Pupusas are El Salvador’s most beloved dish, stuffed with cheese, beans, pork, or a mix of flavors, then cooked to golden perfection on a griddle. Served with curtido (a tangy cabbage slaw) and tomato sauce, they are the perfect comfort food and the heart of Salvadoran cuisine.
